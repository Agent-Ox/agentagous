## The one-liner

@body_lead ElevenLabs makes AI voices: text-to-speech good enough for audiobooks and film, cloning from a short sample, dubbing that keeps the speaker's voice in another language, and, increasingly, conversational agents that answer a phone line and hold the call.

For a creator it is the voice seat in the stack. For an enterprise it is the reason a support line can answer in thirty languages at 3am. For the agentic economy it is the layer that gives every agent in this series a mouth.

## What it actually does

@cap <b>Text-to-speech</b> — Type or paste text; get natural speech in a library voice or your own. The v3 models handle emotion, pacing and emphasis from context and from simple tags, which is what moved it past "robot reading."

@cap <b>Voice cloning</b> — Instant cloning from a minute of audio; professional cloning from more, for a voice indistinguishable from the original. Your own voice, or anyone's who has consented in writing.

@cap <b>Dubbing</b> — A video in one language becomes the same video in another, in the original speaker's voice, with timing preserved. Seventy-plus languages in one pass.

@cap <b>Sound effects and music</b> — Describe a sound, get a sound. Background beds for video without a licence hunt.

@cap <b>ElevenReader</b> — The consumer app: any text, article or book read aloud in a chosen voice. Spotify now uses ElevenLabs to let authors produce audiobooks directly.

@cap <b>ElevenAgents</b> — The enterprise product and the growth engine: voice and chat agents that take calls, answer support, qualify sales leads and run hiring screens, with the testing, monitoring and integrations a call centre needs. Revolut runs support for four million customers on it across the UK and Europe; Klarna put it in front of 35 million Americans.

## The catches

- → <b>Voice rights.</b> Cloning a voice needs documented consent from its owner. Your own is yours; a client's, a colleague's or a celebrity's is not. ElevenLabs verifies professional clones and faces biometric-privacy litigation in the US; the legal exposure for misuse sits with you.
- → <b>Character maths.</b> Long-form audio eats plans fast, and overage on Pro and above is usage-billed. Estimate the month in characters before choosing a tier.
- → <b>Your voices live there.</b> A professional clone is an asset on ElevenLabs' servers. Export the audio you make; the voice itself does not travel.
- → <b>Detection is a moving target.</b> "Can't tell it's AI" cuts both ways. Platforms and regulators are adding disclosure rules for synthetic voice; label it where required.
- → <b>The agent business is not the creator business.</b> Enterprise voice agents are where the company's attention and revenue are going. Creator features keep shipping, but the roadmap is a call centre's.

## Who it is for

@fit <b>Yes</b> — Anyone who publishes spoken content: podcasts, video voice-over, courses, audiobooks, multilingual anything. Buy the Creator tier and stop thinking about voice.

@fit <b>Not yet</b> — Anyone whose use needs a voice they do not own the rights to, or who wants to run everything on their own hardware (open models).

@fit <b>No</b> — Anyone who only needs occasional read-aloud (the free tier, or the voice built into their chat app).

## Why it matters

Every agent in this series types. ElevenLabs is what lets them speak, and the moment an agent can hold a phone call it stops being software people configure and becomes staff people talk to. That is why the fastest-growing part of an audio company is customer support, and why a voice startup founded in 2022 is worth more than most airlines.

## Glossary

@gl <b>ElevenLabs</b> — AI voice company founded 2022 by Mati Staniszewski and Piotr Dąbkowski. Text-to-speech, cloning, dubbing, voice agents. $11B valuation.

@gl <b>Text-to-speech (TTS)</b> — Generating spoken audio from written text.

@gl <b>Voice cloning</b> — Reproducing a specific person's voice from a sample. Instant (short sample) or professional (longer, verified).

@gl <b>Dubbing</b> — Re-voicing spoken content in another language, preserving the original speaker's voice and timing.

@gl <b>ElevenAgents</b> — The enterprise platform for voice and chat agents that take calls and hold conversations.

@gl <b>ElevenReader</b> — The consumer read-aloud app.

@gl <b>Character</b> — The billing unit. Roughly 1,000 characters per minute of speech.

@gl <b>Eleven v3</b> — The current generation of models, with emotion and expressiveness driven by context and tags.

@gl <b>Consent</b> — Written permission from a voice's owner, required before cloning anyone but yourself.
