Nine thousand apps, no code. Watch the task count.

Zapier connects apps to each other without code: when something happens in one (a form is filled, an email arrives, a payment lands), it does something in another (adds a row, sends a message, creates a task). Each connection is a Zap; each step that moves data is a task; you pay for tasks.
